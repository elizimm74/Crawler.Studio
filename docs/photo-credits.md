# Nature photos: sources and licences

The nature photos behind the home page windows, the hero, the final call to action and the
dashboard banner are real photos from Wikimedia Commons. Every one is **public domain or CC0**:
free for commercial use, no credit required. We list them anyway, so we always know where they came from.
No people are shown on purpose (a real face would look like a real customer; ours are simulated).
We did NOT use aaru.com's photos; only their layout idea (a photo behind a product window).
