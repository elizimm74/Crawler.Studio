Paste your website or store link, or drop in your app or game screenshots: onboarding to paywall.

the science behind the customers

Consumer models built on research.

Each simulated customer starts from published studies about how real people browse and buy. Code samples the crowd and counts every answer; AI only plays each person.

questions

Honest answers.

Are these real customers?

No. They are simulated customers: AI playing varied people, built from published research. Crawler shows buying intent and the reasons behind it, not real purchases. Use it before launch, then confirm with real users.

How accurate is it?

We don’t claim a number yet. Crawler is new, and we’re collecting predicted-versus-real results from launches to measure it publicly.

Do you log into my app or game?

No. For apps and games you upload screenshots. For websites, Crawler only visits public pages, and never pays, buys or creates accounts on other people’s sites.
