what we do

Three steps. About a minute.

You bring the product. We bring the crowd.

01

Give us your product

Paste a link to your website or store. For an app or game, upload screenshots. For an ad or TikTok, upload the video.

02

100 simulated customers try it

Different ages, budgets and moods. They look at it like strangers and say yes, maybe or no, with a reason and a one-line quote.

03

You get a ranked fix list

What stopped people, where they left, and which fixes would win back the most. Then test again to see who changed their mind.

why we do it

Launching blind is a guess.

Most small products launch without ever seeing what a stranger sees. Real user testing takes weeks and costs money. Friends are too polite to tell you the price is confusing.

We wanted an honest first read in a minute, before you spend on ads, a launch day or a month of work.

the reality

~6% Most visitors leave without paying. The median subscription app turns about 6% of downloads into a trial. Source: RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2025.

why you need us

You know your product too well to see it.

See it like a stranger

You can’t un-know what your product does. Simulated first-time visitors haven’t read your mind, and they tell you what’s unclear.

Find the one thing costing you most

Fixes are ranked by how many customers each could win back, counted from the results, not guessed.

Fix it before you pay for traffic

Ads sent to a leaky page are wasted money. Find the leak first, for a fraction of the cost of a real study.

Test again with the same customers

Changed your price or headline? Re-run the same crowd and see exactly who changed their mind.

Honest about what this is

  • It is a fast, low-cost first read, built on public research, with every assumption labelled.
  • It isn’t real customers, and it can’t promise sales. Simulated customers show buying intent, not real purchases.
